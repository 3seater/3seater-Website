# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
3seater is a designer's visual work archive. Visitors explore real graphic design, motion and 3D projects. It is a showcase, not a sales site.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Preserve Work, Archive and Index navigation, all projects, project media, metadata, client collections, category/year filtering and contact links. Never invent portfolio facts or add sales copy.

## Brand Commitments
Interface: black, white, neutral off-white/off-black and grayscale only. Artwork, videos and thumbnails must retain their original colors. Use sans-serif typography throughout, with genuinely different font families across directions; do not repeat the same font system in every version. Minimal, creative, tech-aware, noncorporate. iOS-inspired material and controls are allowed where appropriate.

## Current Direction — Fresh Batch Two
Build twenty new complete portfolio experiences from scratch. Preserve the factual content and page capabilities, not the previous compositions, header/footer systems, or visual hierarchy. The existing website, the first twenty explorations, Reuben Timothy and Sturdy must not be the primary design templates for this batch.

Prioritize breadth of work in the first viewport. Avoid oversized homepage media, two giant videos filling the screen, large introductory heroes and huge wordmarks. Desktop homepages should expose at least six identifiable projects within a typical 1280×800 viewport, usually more. Mobile should keep work compact and navigable without tiny unreadable labels. Rearrange content creatively through different organization models, navigation placement, grouping, interactions and spatial rhythm. Changing only columns, colors or a font does not constitute a fresh direction.

The grayscale requirement applies only to interface chrome. Never desaturate the actual portfolio content.

## Evidence on Hand
src/site.json, dist/data.json and the user's Work directory are authoritative. Existing site uses Arial/Helvetica and SF Mono/Menlo/Consolas fallback stacks. Thirty projects currently exist.

## Requested Work
Twenty fresh full-site directions in a second batch, each with a direct viewable link and functioning Work, Archive, Index, project and client pages. Selection happens after implementation. Keep batch one available separately for comparison. No replacement of the original production portfolio is requested.
